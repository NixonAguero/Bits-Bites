Crema de Pejibaye

Pejibaye cream is a sweet, creamy alcoholic beverage made from pejibaye, a traditional Costa Rican fruit that is very common in the Turrialba area. Its flavor is smooth, slightly sweet, and with a thick texture, making it an ideal choice as a digestif or a special drink for celebrations. It is a preparation that blends the traditional with the artisanal, highlighting the native flavors of the region.

History

Pejibaye cream is a sweet, creamy alcoholic drink from Costa Rica, made from pejibaye, a pre-Columbian staple fruit from Turrialba. It's a modern twist on tradition: smooth, slightly sweet, thick, and perfect as a digestif or for celebrations. Ancestral flavor, contemporary spirit.
